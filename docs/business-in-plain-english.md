# Axiom in plain English (for non–insurance partners)

Short explanation you can use when someone does not know the space or the product.

---

## One sentence

Car insurers need to decide who is at fault after a crash. Today that often means a person watching dashcam video and reading traffic laws. We use AI to do that first pass in minutes, flag disagreements, and help them pay the right amount and defend their decisions.

---

## The mental model (30 seconds)

1. **After a crash, money moves.** The insurer (or the fleet paying its own claims) has to set **fault** and **reserves** — who pays whom and how much to set aside.
2. **Evidence is a mess** — video, photos, police PDFs, statements. **Humans** are the bottleneck: slow, expensive, and inconsistent.
3. **We** turn that evidence into a **clear liability readout** fast, with **multiple AI checks** so one bad guess does not become policy. Humans still **decide**; we **accelerate and standardize** the work.

---

## Why insurance (in one line)

Insurance is a business of paying claims — and **paying wrong** (too much or too inconsistent) is a huge cost. Your partner only needs to know: **speed and consistency in fault decisions = money and risk.**

## Jargon swap

| If you say | They may not follow | Try instead |
|------------|--------------------|-------------|
| TPA / carrier | (unclear) | Companies that process auto claims (insurers or their outsourced shops) |
| Liability / leakage | (vague) | Who was at fault / paying more than you should on claims |
| Statute alignment | (unclear) | Tie what happened to the actual traffic law in that state |
| Multi-model consensus | (tech flex) | Three independent AIs, then a check when they do not agree — we do not hide when we are unsure |

---

## What to emphasize (business, not product)

- **Who pays you:** B2B — claims teams, insurers, big fleets — not consumers buying an app.
- **What you sell:** **Time** (liability view in minutes vs days) + **consistency** + **paper trail** for audits and disputes.
- **Why it is defensible:** The problem is real and budgeted; you are not inventing a new habit — you are **unblocking an expensive pipeline**.

---

## Optional analogy

**Triage in an ER, but for car-crash files:** the AI reads the file first, highlights what matters, and sends unclear cases to the doctor — but here the “doctor” is the adjuster and the “hospital” is the claims department.

---

## If they still zone out

Ask one question: **When two people watch the same crash video, do you think they always agree on who is at fault?** The answer is no — and that is the wedge: inconsistent human judgment is expensive; Axiom standardizes the first pass and documents it.
