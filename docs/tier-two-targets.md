# Axiom — Tier 2 (regional) ICPs & LinkedIn company pages

**What “Tier 2” means here**

Organizations that are **strong fits for Axiom** (auto + commercial auto + claims ops you can actually reach) but are **one step down from the Tier 1 list** in one or more of:

- **Geographic footprint** — single-region or multistate, not fully national
- **Employee count** — often **~300–2,000** in claims/ops (faster decisions than enterprise procurement)
- **Line mix** — P&C and auto are material, but not always “billion-dollar auto” scale
- **Channel** — more **independent agent** and **regional TPA** than mega-TPO programs

**Why prioritize Tier 2 in early GTM:** shorter cycles, more accessible **VP / AVP / Director of Claims** titles, and often **dedicated innovation or transformation** leaders who can sponsor a **shadow-audit pilot** without a 9-month RFP.

**Pair with** [`docs/tier-one-targets.md`](./tier-one-targets.md) (mega TPA / national carrier list — Tier 1).

**How to use**

- Same as Tier 1: open each **About** to confirm the page matches the carrier (name collisions are common, e.g. “Merchants” vs payment companies).
- Prefer canonical URLs: `https://www.linkedin.com/company/{slug}/`
- A small number of slugs are **inferred** from posts/jobs; if a link 404s, search the **exact legal name** on LinkedIn.

## G. Where Tier 1 ends and Tier 2 begins (rule of thumb)

| Signal | More Tier **1** | More Tier **2** |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Employee count (insurance/claims org) | 5,000+ or global TPA | 300–2,500 |
| Procurement / InfoSec / RFP | Heavy | Lighter; pilot via **innovation** or **line-of-business** |
| Auto as % of book | One of top 2 lines | Material but regional |
| Time to first meeting | 8–20 touches | 3–10 touches (often) |
