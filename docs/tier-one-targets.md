# Axiom — Tier 1 ICPs & LinkedIn Company Pages

**Purpose:** A working map of high-value prospect organizations for Axiom: organizations with **substantial auto (or auto-heavy commercial) liability and claims volume**, a **reason to use dashcam / multi-evidence / shadow-audit reporting**, and **buyer roles you can actually reach** (VP Claims, CCO, TPA program leads) within **~12–18 month** sales cycles.

**How to use this list**
- **Primary targets first:** mega-TPAs and high-volume P&C writers with commercial & personal auto.
- **Verify before bulk outreach:** LinkedIn company URLs occasionally change, merge, or get regional mirrors (`mx.linkedin.com` vs `www.linkedin.com` — the slug is the same; prefer `https://www.linkedin.com/company/{slug}/`).
- **De-dupe:** e.g. York Risk Services is now part of **Sedgwick** — do not run parallel campaigns to both.
- **Procurement note:** Firms with 2,000+ employees often have formal vendor onboarding; your **first win** is more likely a **regional carrier, mid-market TPA, or a pilot with a specific auto-heavy program** than a top-5 global TPA in month one.

**Regional (Tier 2) list:** For super-regional mutuals, sub-national footprints, and faster pilot cycles, see [`docs/tier-two-targets.md`](./tier-two-targets.md).

**Legend**
| Tag | Meaning |
|-----|--------|
| **TPA** | Third-party claims administrator (high auto / GL / WC volume) |
| **Carrier (auto-heavy)** | Personal and/or commercial auto is a material line |
| **Fleet / captive** | Self-insured or large fleet with internal claims or TPA-sourced adjusters |
| **Specialty** | E&S, MGAs, programs with transportation focus — good for *commercial auto* and dashcam use cases |

## How to find *people* (not just companies) in Sales Nav

- **Titles:** *Chief Claims Officer*, *VP Claims*, *VP of Claims*, *AVP Auto*, *AVP Commercial Claims*, *VP Casualty*, *TPA Operations*, *Claims Transformation*, *Claims Innovation*, *Litigation* (for your discovery narrative)
- **Filters:** 50–1,000 employees in **Insurance** + **US** = often the sweet spot for a **60–90 day pilot** decision.

---

## Manual verification before you add to a sequencer

1. Open each **LinkedIn company** URL; confirm the **About** section matches the organization you intend (names collide, e.g. “Crawford Company” = HVAC, not **Crawford & Company** the adjuster).
2. Prefer **one canonical** URL: `https://www.linkedin.com/company/{slug}/` (trailing slash optional).
3. If a company has **rebranded** (merger, holding company rename), search the new name and update the slug.
